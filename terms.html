1. Normal orders

Cinnamon rolls, spring rolls, breakfast and lunch packs, and other menu items.

  1. 3 days' notice. Orders must be placed at least 3 days before the delivery or pick-up date. For example, an order placed on Saturday can be delivered from Tuesday. Earlier dates cannot be chosen on the website.
  2. Urgent orders. If you need something sooner, message us on WhatsApp. Urgent orders depend on availability and are not guaranteed.
  3. Choosing your date. You choose your delivery or pick-up date at checkout.
  4. Choosing your time. After you confirm your payment, you choose a 1-hour time window between on your chosen date. The window is the time we aim to deliver or have your order ready; we confirm the final timing with you on WhatsApp.
  5. Payment first. Orders are paid in full in advance (see Payment). We start preparing your order only after we have confirmed that your payment has been received. Time windows are not held for unpaid orders.
  6. Prices. Prices are in Ghana cedis (GH₵) as shown on the website when you order.
  7. Delivery and pick-up. Delivery fees are not included in the order total. They depend on your location and are agreed with you on WhatsApp. Pick-up has no delivery fee; we share the pick-up point on WhatsApp.
  8. Changes. To change your date, time or order, message us on WhatsApp with your order reference as early as possible. Changes depend on availability.

2. Events & catering

Party and event packages, breakfast event packages and bulk catering.

  1. 2 weeks' notice. Events must be booked at least 2 weeks (14 days) before the event date. Earlier dates cannot be chosen on the website.
  2. Enquiry first. Send an enquiry through the website. We confirm availability for your date and send you an invoice before any payment is made.
  3. Booking deposit. A booking becomes valid when at least 40% of the selected package has been paid.
  4. Full payment. Payment in full must be completed at least 14 working days before the event. For bookings made closer to the event than that, full payment is due when the booking is confirmed.
  5. Price changes. Package prices may change, typically after 2 to 3 months, because of changes in product prices. To avoid any increase, full payment upfront is recommended.
  6. Transport. Transportation cost is not included in any package. It is quoted once you share the event location.
  7. Set-up. Breakfast event packages include a food set-up station. Optional extras (such as truss, lighting, floral works, backdrops and drink bar stands) are charged separately as listed.
  8. Cancellation. No refund is issued on cancellation, except where the cancellation arises from a force majeure event, in which case 40% of the amount paid is refunded.

3. Payment

  1. Methods. We accept Telecel Cash, MTN MoMo and bank transfer. The account details are shown on the payment page after you place your order, and all accounts are in the name Marvelyn Boakye Yiadom.
  2. Use your reference. Always use your order or booking reference (for example BAF-… or EVT-…) when paying, so we can match your payment.
  3. Confirmation. An order or booking is confirmed only after we have checked that the payment has arrived in our account.
  4. Stay safe. We will never ask for your PIN. Only pay to the details shown on our website.
