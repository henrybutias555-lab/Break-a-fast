How ordering works

Complete your order on the site first. You'll get an order reference, and then you pay using that reference, so we can match your payment to your order.

  1. Pick your treatsChoose flavours and package sizes, then add them to your cart.
  2. Enter your detailsName, phone, and delivery or pick-up.
  3. Review & place orderYou receive an order reference such as BAF-7K2QX.
  4. Pay your wayTelecel Cash, MTN MoMo or bank transfer. Send the exact amount with your order reference.
  5. Confirm on WhatsAppSend us your transaction ID in one tap. We verify and get baking!
