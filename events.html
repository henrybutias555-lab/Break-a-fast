    How booking works

    1. Send your enquiryPick a package and guest count above, then send it to us on WhatsApp. You'll get a booking reference.
    2. We confirm & invoiceWe confirm your date and send an invoice, including transport for your location.
    3. Pay a 40% token to bookYour booking is valid once at least 40% is paid by Telecel Cash, MTN MoMo or bank transfer, using your booking reference.
    4. Pay the balanceFull payment is due at least 14 working days before the event.
    5. Celebrate!We set up, serve and make it memorable.
    Please wait for our confirmation before sending your 40% token, so your payment is matched to a confirmed date.

    Terms & conditions

    • Events must be booked at least 2 weeks before the event date.
    • Bookings become valid when the client has paid at least 40% of the selected package.
    • Payment in full must be completed at least 14 working days before delivery. For bookings made closer to the event than that, full payment is due when the booking is confirmed.
    • Package prices may change over time due to changes in product prices. To avoid any increases, full payment upfront is recommended.
    • No refund is issued on cancellation, except where cancellation arises from a force majeure event, in which case 40% of the amount paid is refunded.
    • Transportation cost is not included in any package.
